# TODO:VINCERE! — Piazza del Sapere UNISA

> Project developed by **TODO:VINCERE!** for **Geckode UNISA powered by Youbiquo**, in the **NEOLAiA** category.

## Overview

**Piazza del Sapere UNISA** is an immersive digital environment designed to support university students throughout their learning journey.  
Our contribution extends an already existing environment by introducing a new feature composed of two complementary spaces:

- a **Tutoring Room**, where students can interact with an **AI tutor**;
- a **Mindfulness Station**, designed to promote relaxation, cognitive recovery, and well-being.

This feature was conceived to enrich the original project by combining academic support and student well-being within the same digital experience.

The goal is **not** to simulate an exam or assess the student, but to provide **non-judgmental academic guidance**, inspired by the role of a professor during tutoring sessions: a supportive presence that helps clarify doubts, encourages reasoning, and promotes deeper understanding.

---

## Our Added Feature

The feature we added to the existing **Piazza del Sapere UNISA** project introduces two new interactive spaces aimed at improving the student experience:

### 1. Tutoring Room
A dedicated space where students can engage with an **AI tutor** acting as an academic guide.  
Rather than evaluating performance, the tutor supports the student by:

- explaining concepts clearly and progressively;
- guiding reasoning and reflection;
- answering questions in a supportive and accessible way;
- helping students study without pressure or judgment.

At the current stage, the tutoring experience focuses on the **Computer Science** area, specifically on the following subjects:

- **Software Engineering**
- **Operating Systems**
- **Computer Networks**

### 2. Mindfulness Station
A second area designed to complement the tutoring experience by supporting mental well-being.  
This station allows students to take a break, relax, and reduce mental overload, reinforcing the idea that effective learning and well-being should be treated as connected dimensions.

Together, these two additions are intended to make the original environment more supportive, balanced, and student-centered.

---

## Context of Use

The project is designed for the university context and specifically targets students who need:

- continuous support during study activities;
- help with complex topics;
- a reassuring and accessible learning environment;
- tools to manage stress and cognitive overload.

It is integrated into the existing **Piazza del Sapere UNISA** environment and expands its purpose: not only as a symbolic place of knowledge, but also as a digital space for guidance, support, and well-being.

---

## Problem Addressed

University students often face challenges that are not only related to the complexity of academic content, but also to the lack of accessible, personalized, and always-available support.

The main issues this feature aims to address include:

- difficulty understanding complex concepts independently;
- lack of continuous academic guidance;
- high **cognitive load** caused by dense or poorly structured materials;
- stress, mental fatigue, and reduced concentration;
- lack of integration between academic performance and personal well-being.

This feature was therefore designed to respond to a dual need:  
to **support learning** and to **promote mental well-being** at the same time.

---

## Proposed Solution

The solution introduces two complementary spaces into the immersive environment:

### Tutoring Room
A study-support space where the student interacts with an **AI tutor** designed to:

- guide reasoning;
- explain concepts clearly and progressively;
- support content re-elaboration;
- provide assistance in a **non-judgmental** way;
- respect the student’s pace and needs.

### Mindfulness Station
A space dedicated to emotional regulation and recovery, designed to:

- promote relaxation and decompression;
- reduce stress and mental tension;
- improve focus and continuity in studying;
- integrate well-being as part of the learning process.

---

## Design Intentions

This feature was designed with a **human-centered** approach, where technology, learning, and well-being coexist within the same environment.

The main design intentions were:

- to provide an accessible and personalized tutoring experience;
- to create a safe, welcoming, and non-evaluative space;
- to integrate study support and well-being into one digital experience;
- to support students both cognitively and emotionally;
- to design an interaction that is simple, clear, and sustainable over time.

---

## Usability Principles Applied

The solution follows key **usability** and **Human-Computer Interaction** principles, with particular attention to the following:

### Cognitive Load Reduction
- progressively organized content;
- simple and focused interactions;
- clear presentation of information;
- guided support without overwhelming the user.

### Consistency and Predictability
- recognizable environments and interaction patterns;
- coherent system behavior;
- intuitive navigation.

### Immediate and Understandable Feedback
- clear and contextual responses;
- continuous support during interaction;
- reduced uncertainty.

### User Control and Freedom
- the ability to proceed at one’s own pace;
- an adaptable experience based on student needs;
- interaction that is never forced or pressuring.

### Accessibility and Simplicity
- understandable language;
- readable structure;
- an inclusive experience for students with different levels of preparation.

### Non-Judgmental Approach
- the AI tutor does not assess or penalize;
- the environment encourages exploration, questioning, and learning;
- mistakes are treated as opportunities for understanding.
